Biography

Jaymes65 (Jaymes Manyange) is a dynamic DJ from Centurion, Gauteng, South Africa, who has been captivating audiences since 2022 with his eclectic mix of Afro-House, Amapiano, Deep House, and Afro Beats. His unique ability to blend soulful Afro sounds with energetic house beats has earned him recognition on diverse stages across South Africa.

Known for reading the crowd and delivering memorable experiences, Jaymes65 specializes in blending Afro House with touches of Hip Hop and other genres, creating a sound that's both authentic and innovative. His sets are characterized by seamless transitions, infectious rhythms, and an energy that keeps dance floors packed until the early hours.

From intimate venues to bustling nightlife spots, Jaymes65 has performed at some of South Africa's most popular establishments including News Café, The Palms Backyard, Cubana Fourways, and R55 Chill. His ability to connect with diverse audiences through music has made him a rising star in the South African music scene.

With a passion for pushing boundaries and exploring new sounds, Jaymes65 continues to make his mark in the industry, bringing fresh energy to every performance and creating unforgettable musical experiences that celebrate the rich tapestry of African music culture.
